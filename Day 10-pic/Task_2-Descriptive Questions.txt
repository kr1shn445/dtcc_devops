1. Stock Market Trading
Scenario: A trading firm notices unusual volatility in a stock's price.

- Descriptive Analysis (What happened?)
The stock price dropped by 8% within two trading hours, while trading volume increased to three times its usual level.

- Diagnostic Analysis (Why did it happen?)
The drop may have been caused by disappointing earnings, negative market news, or large institutional sell orders.

- Predictive Analysis (What might happen next?)
The stock may experience continued volatility or further price declines if selling pressure persists.

- Prescriptive Analysis (What shall we do?)
Monitor market news, reassess risk exposure, set appropriate stop-loss limits, and avoid making decisions based solely on short-term price movements.

2. Retail Store Inventory Management
Scenario: A supermarket repeatedly runs out of essential products despite having sufficient overall inventory.

- Descriptive Analysis:
The store experienced 15 stockouts of high-demand products in one month, while other products remained unsold in storage.

- Diagnostic Analysis:
Stockouts occurred because demand forecasts were inaccurate and inventory was distributed unevenly across product categories.

- Predictive Analysis:
Popular products may run out again during weekends and holidays if current replenishment practices continue.

- Prescriptive Analysis:
Set product-specific reorder points, maintain safety stock for fast-moving items, and adjust replenishment schedules using sales trends.

3. Network Traffic and Cybersecurity
Scenario: A company's network experiences a sudden increase in inbound traffic.

- Descriptive Analysis:
Incoming network traffic increased fivefold within ten minutes, and server response times deteriorated.

- Diagnostic Analysis:
Investigation reveals that thousands of requests are arriving from many sources, potentially indicating a distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) attack.

- Predictive Analysis:
If the traffic continues, the servers may become unavailable and legitimate users may lose access.

- Prescriptive Analysis:
Activate DDoS protection, apply rate limiting, filter malicious traffic, scale resources where appropriate, and monitor network logs.

4. Manufacturing and Equipment Maintenance
Scenario: A factory's production line experiences increasingly frequent machine failures.

- Descriptive Analysis:
Machine downtime increased from 3 hours to 12 hours per week, reducing production output by 20%.

- Diagnostic Analysis:
Sensor readings reveal rising operating temperatures and unusual vibration levels, suggesting bearing wear or inadequate lubrication.

- Predictive Analysis:
The machine may experience a major failure soon if the abnormal readings continue.

- Prescriptive Analysis:
Schedule preventive maintenance, replace worn components, lubricate the equipment, and use 
sensor-based alerts to identify future problems early.

5. Banking and Fraud Detection
Scenario: A bank identifies an unusual pattern of customer transactions.

- Descriptive Analysis:
A customer account records 25 transactions within five minutes, totaling ₹1.5 lakh, unlike its usual activity.

- Diagnostic Analysis:
The transactions originated from unfamiliar devices and locations, suggesting possible account compromise or fraudulent activity.

- Predictive Analysis:
Similar transactions may continue if the account remains compromised.

- Prescriptive Analysis:
Apply the bank's fraud-risk checks, temporarily restrict suspicious transactions where policy permits, verify the customer's identity, and secure the account if fraud is confirmed.
